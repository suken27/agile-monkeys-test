RabbitMQ auto-delete semantics: a queue is deleted once its consumer count drops to zero, provided it has had at least one consumer. RabbitTemplate.receive(queue, timeout) subscribes a temporary consumer and cancels it right after (whether it got a message or timed out). JUnit doesn't guarantee test method execution order here, so whichever test runs first does a receive() call that subscribes-then-cancels — triggering RabbitMQ to auto-delete the queue since it just had its first-ever consumer drop to zero.
